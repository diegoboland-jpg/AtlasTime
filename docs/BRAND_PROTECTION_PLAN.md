# Kikroo brand and product-protection plan

**Prepared:** 2026-08-17
**Status:** preliminary preparation; no application filed and no legal clearance claimed.

## What can be protected

- **Trademark:** the distinctive `KIKROO` name and potentially the rooster/world-clock logo for selected goods and services.
- **Copyright:** original code, copy, documentation, and sufficiently original artwork. Copyright protects the expression, not the underlying idea or scheduling method.
- **Confidential information:** unreleased roadmap, secrets, signing material, private operational procedures, and genuinely confidential know-how.
- **Contracts:** assignments and confidentiality obligations for external contributors.

The app idea — comparing time zones to find a humane meeting time — is not protected merely by writing it down or publishing the app. Competitive protection therefore comes from the registered brand, execution quality, protected creative/code assets, distribution, reputation, and carefully controlled confidential information.

## Filing decision gates

- [ ] legal applicant: individual or company;
- [ ] evidence that the applicant is entitled to the name/logo assets;
- [ ] first territory and next 24-month target markets;
- [ ] approved list of goods/services;
- [ ] word mark versus combined mark strategy;
- [ ] live similarity search reviewed by a qualified professional;
- [ ] filing budget, monitoring owner, and renewal calendar.

## Candidate classification discussion

The likely starting points are:

- **Class 9:** downloadable mobile application/software;
- **Class 42:** software as a service and related hosted software services.

These are candidates, not final legal classifications. Do not add Class 38 merely because the app helps people communicate; include a class only when the actual goods/services and commercial plan justify it. Confirm the wording against the current INPI/Nice lists or with a trademark professional.

## Brand-use rules before registration

- Use `Kikroo`, not `KIKROO®`; the registered symbol must not be used before registration.
- Keep one approved logo master and documented variants.
- Do not let third parties redraw, recolor, or distribute the logo without written permission.
- Do not describe a domain, company name, or app-store listing as proof of trademark ownership.
- Keep public naming consistent while clearance is underway; escalate any credible conflict before increasing marketing spend.
