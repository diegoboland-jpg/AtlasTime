# Kikroo protected-beta tester recruitment plan

**Starting position (2026-08-17):** three people approached; one completed a useful test.

## Success is completion, not invitation

Use two short waves rather than waiting for 5–10 volunteers at once.

### Wave 0 — repair the test invitation

- Reinvite the current three people with a single 15–20 minute mission and a firm date.
- Target: at least **two completed** sessions A–C, including the existing completed tester where evidence is sufficient.
- Ask one question after non-completion: “What stopped you: time, installation, unclear task, privacy concern, or technical failure?”
- Improve the instructions before recruiting more people.

### Wave 1 — protected beta gate

- Recruit 3–5 additional people, one at a time or in pairs.
- Target: at least **five completed distinct testers**, not merely five invitations.
- Required coverage: primary Android phone, a different Android device/version, one iPhone, and one desktop browser.
- A tester counts as completed after sessions A–C and one structured feedback record, even when the result is “no defect found.”

## Low-friction mission

1. Install/open Kikroo from the approved link.
2. Add two sample places or people in different time zones.
3. Use **Find a good time** and return with **Now**.
4. Close and reopen the app; confirm the group remains.
5. Prepare one feedback report from **About & privacy**, even if the report says everything worked.

Calendar connection and availability sharing remain optional follow-up sessions. Do not make them a condition for the first completed test.

## Follow-up cadence

- Invitation: provide the mission, deadline, anonymous tester ID, approved URL, privacy/support contact, and expected duration.
- After 48 hours: one short reminder with the same link.
- At the deadline: close the request politely; record completed, declined, or no response. Do not repeatedly pursue people.

## Recruitment channels

Start with people who naturally coordinate across time zones: remote colleagues, friends/family abroad, frequent travelers, freelancers, and small international teams. Avoid collecting contact lists in the repository. Store the invitation/contact tracker in protected company storage.
